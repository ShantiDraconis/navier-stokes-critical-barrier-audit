# 09 — Spatial Concentration

Ansatz:
\[
u(x,t)\sim A(t)U((x-x_*)/\ell(t)),\quad A\sim(T-t)^{-\alpha},\ \ell\sim(T-t)^\beta.\]

Scaling diagnostics:
\[
\|u\|_2^2\sim A^2\ell^3 \Rightarrow 3\beta\ge 2\alpha,
\]
\[
\|u\|_3^3\sim A^3\ell^3,\ L^3\text{ divergence requires }\alpha>\beta.
\]

Window:
\[
\frac{2}{3}\alpha\le\beta<\alpha.
\]
