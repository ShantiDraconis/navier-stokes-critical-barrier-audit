# 06 — Forcing Framework

Starts from
\[
f=\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nu\Delta u+\nabla p.
\]

For local ansatz
\[
u(x,t)=A(t)U\!\left(\frac{x-x_*}{\ell(t)}\right),\quad
A(t)\sim(T-t)^{-\alpha},\ \ell(t)\sim(T-t)^\beta,
\]
track separate scaling of each term and required cancellations for smooth forcing claims.
