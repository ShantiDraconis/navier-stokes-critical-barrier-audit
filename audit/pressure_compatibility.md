# Pressure Compatibility Audit

Core equation:
\[
-\Delta p=\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j)-\nabla\cdot f.
\]

Check compatibility chain:
\[
\text{concentration of }u
\leftrightarrow u_i u_j
\leftrightarrow p
\leftrightarrow \nabla p
\leftrightarrow f.
\]

Focus: nonlocal constraints and support/elliptic consistency under proposed concentration patterns.
