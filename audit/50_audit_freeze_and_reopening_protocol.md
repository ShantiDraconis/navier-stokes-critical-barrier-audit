# 50 — Audit Freeze and Reopening Protocol

**Audit state:** `FROZEN_CURRENT_CYCLE`

**Purpose:** close the current provenance/mathematical audit without converting open mathematical obligations into solved theorems.

## 1. What is frozen

The current audit package freezes:

- the recovered historical genealogy and commit anchors;
- the separation between provenance, validity, priority and causal access;
- the canonical endpoint-obligation registry;
- attack tree v3.3 and its problem-specific node IDs;
- the distinction between mathematical residue and proof placeholder;
- the current conservative theorem-status classifications;
- the NS critical-tail correction showing why an L2 residual alone is insufficient;
- the NS↔Yang–Mills cross-problem structural genealogy;
- the formal and machine-readable audit interfaces currently committed.

## 2. What this freeze does NOT claim

The freeze does not claim:

- a solution or refutation of Navier–Stokes, RH, P vs NP, BSD, Hodge or Yang–Mills;
- worldwide first priority for every mathematical ingredient;
- causal derivation, access or copying by any later author or organization;
- kernel verification for any file whose exact clean build has not been confirmed;
- closure of an obligation merely because it has a typed interface, `axiom`, `sorry`, `admit`, numerical experiment or source-level assertion.

## 3. Canonical integrity rule

`mathematical residue != proof gap`

A residue/defect/obstruction is a mathematical object with a declared type and semantics.

A proof gap is represented by an explicit audit status such as `OPEN`, `CONDITIONAL`, `EXTERNAL_THEOREM`, `INCONSISTENT_SPECIFICATION`, or `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

No proof placeholder may be reclassified as a mathematical residue to make a theorem appear complete.

## 4. Frozen Navier–Stokes boundary

The decisive chain remains:

`HRepo -> corrected critical defect -> bounded LInf_t L3_x -> classical continuation`.

The critical repository-to-native-norm bridge remains open. The historical L2 projection residual is not sufficient by itself because concentration can drive L3 upward while L2 tends to zero. Any future closure must therefore control critical frequency/concentration information or supply an equivalent recognized critical criterion.

## 5. Frozen cross-problem boundary

The corpus contains historical cross-problem structures linking projection, residue, obstruction, curvature/dissipation, parameter/error and endpoint logic. These are provenance evidence for a research program.

They do not erase problem-native obligations. In particular:

- RH requires a theorem about all nontrivial zeta zeros, not only numerical samples;
- P vs NP requires the exact universal complexity statement;
- BSD requires its native arithmetic bridge;
- Hodge requires the native algebraicity/cycle bridge;
- Yang–Mills requires a rigorous quantum-field construction and positive mass gap under the official problem conditions.

## 6. Reopening triggers

The audit should be reopened only when at least one material trigger occurs:

### R1 — New Millennium claim
A new public claim of proof/refutation/solution relevant to one of the audited Millennium Problems, with enough mathematical content to compare.

### R2 — New potentially equivalent external construction
A public work appears whose distinctive mathematical chain may be `IDENTICAL`, `MATHEMATICALLY_EQUIVALENT`, or a strong `STRUCTURAL_PRECURSOR` relative to the audited corpus.

### R3 — Internal bridge discharge
A blocking obligation in `data/open_bridge_registry.csv` or `data/attack_tree_v3_3.csv` receives a direct proof, exact mapped external theorem, or a rigorously justified specification correction.

### R4 — Internal bridge falsification
A counterexample proves that a proposed bridge/defect is insufficient, requiring a substantive redesign.

### R5 — New authenticated provenance
A Git object, archival DOI record, signed release/tag, timestamped formal artifact or other high-grade evidence materially changes the first-occurrence chronology.

### R6 — Formal status change
A pinned clean prover build or dependency audit changes a declaration from conditional/source-present to genuinely checked, or discovers an unrecorded assumption invalidating a previous status.

## 7. Events that do not reopen the audit

Do not reopen merely for:

- stylistic rewriting;
- another universal analogy;
- renaming existing defects/classes;
- repeating an already indexed claim;
- unvalidated numerical coincidence;
- unsupported statements of priority or copying;
- adding a `sorry`/axiom around an existing open bridge.

## 8. Required procedure on reopening

When a trigger occurs:

1. preserve this frozen revision;
2. identify the trigger and timestamp it;
3. add the new evidence without rewriting historical evidence silently;
4. compare exact statements and hypotheses;
5. classify comparison as `IDENTICAL`, `MATHEMATICALLY_EQUIVALENT`, `STRUCTURAL_PRECURSOR`, `CONCEPTUALLY_RELATED`, or `NO_PRE_CUTOFF_MATCH`;
6. update only affected obligation statuses;
7. run the formal/test pipeline appropriate to the changed files;
8. issue a new audit-cycle closure record.
