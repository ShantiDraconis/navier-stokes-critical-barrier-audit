# Limitations

- Priority evidence establishes chronology, not causal derivation.
- Private-repository metadata may be partially inaccessible; unresolved fields remain `UNKNOWN`.
- Scaling ansatz checks are diagnostic and do not by themselves constitute full PDE nonexistence/existence theorems.
- Numerical diagnostics (including means like \(\bar\delta_h\)) do not imply universal global regularity.
- Conjectures (PMC/MLC) remain unproved until formal proof artifacts are provided.
