\begin{abstract}
We present a reproducible audit architecture for a multi-repository research program on three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes regularity and related error-parameter models. The contribution of this article is methodological rather than a claim of resolution of the Clay Millennium problem. We reconstruct a timestamped development path from collapse/regularity formulations through projection--residual decompositions, residual correction dynamics, parameter gaps, weighted and sensitivity-based error decompositions, vector-valued error coordinates, phase models, and later critical-space/noncircular analysis. We formalize elementary internal lemmas in multiple proof assistants, introduce machine-readable provenance and claim-status graphs, and provide executable tests and counterexamples to invalid inference patterns. A Navier--Stokes diagnostic ratio
\[
\varepsilon_{NS}(u)=\frac{\|(u\cdot\nabla)u\|_{L^2}}{\nu\|\Delta u\|_{L^2}}
\]
is shown to be invariant under the classical three-dimensional Navier--Stokes scaling whenever defined. We identify the decisive unresolved bridge: no result presently established by this audit proves that control or vanishing of the repository-defined error state implies a recognized critical regularity criterion such as bounded $L^\infty_tL^3_x$. The audit therefore distinguishes reproducible research infrastructure and internally proved identities from unresolved PDE implications.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Long mathematical research programs often mix theorem statements, heuristic models, numerical evidence, proof-assistant experiments, repository history, and later reconstructions. This makes retrospective evaluation difficult. We address the problem by treating provenance and mathematical validity as distinct axes.

The present corpus contains a documented Navier--Stokes research line beginning in late 2025, followed by projection/residual and error-parameter frameworks and later critical-space analyses. Our objective is to reconstruct this path without using chronology as a substitute for proof.

\paragraph{Scope.} This paper does not claim a proof of global regularity or finite-time singularity for three-dimensional Navier--Stokes. Instead, it provides an auditable framework in which such a claim would have to discharge explicit bridge obligations.

\section{Classical setting}
We consider the incompressible Navier--Stokes equations
\begin{align}
\partial_tu +(u\cdot\nabla)u + \nabla p &= \nu\Delta u + f,\\
\nabla\cdot u &=0,
\end{align}
with the precise domain, forcing and solution class required to be declared for each theorem. The classical scaling on $\mathbb R^3$ is
\[
u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u(\lambda x,\lambda^2t).
\]
Critical quantities are invariant under this transformation.

The audit reconstructs the research path

\section{Error parameterization}
Let a problem state be described by parameters $\theta=(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n)$ and targets $\theta^*$. Define nonnegative gaps
\[
\Delta_i=d_i(\theta_i,\theta_i^*).
\]
A weighted scalarization is
\[
E_w=\sum_iw_i g_i(\Delta_i),\qquad w_i\ge0,\quad\sum_iw_i=1.
\]
For $E_w>0$ define
\[
C_i^{(w)}=100\frac{w_i g_i(\Delta_i)}{E_w}.
\]
\begin{lemma}[Normalization]
If $E_w>0$, then $\sum_iC_i^{(w)}=100$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\[
\sum_iC_i^{(w)}=\frac{100}{E_w}\sum_iw_ig_i(\Delta_i)=100.
\]
\end{proof}
This proves normalization, not canonicity of the coordinates or weights.

\subsection{Sensitivity decomposition}
For $s_i=|\partial E/\partial\theta_i|$, a local alternative is
\[
C_i^{(s)}=100\frac{s_i\Delta_i}{\sum_js_j\Delta_j},
\]
when the denominator is positive. This model is not generally equivalent to fixed structural weighting.

\section{Error-coordinate geometry}
Represent the unresolved state by
\[
\varepsilon(P)=(\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_n)\in\mathbb R_+^n,
\qquad
\|\varepsilon(P)\|_2=\left(\sum_i\varepsilon_i^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
\begin{lemma}
$\|\varepsilon\|_2=0$ iff every coordinate is zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All squares are nonnegative, so their finite sum is zero exactly when each square is zero.
\end{proof}
For nonzero initial magnitude define
\[
\mathcal P_k=100\left(1-\frac{\|\varepsilon^{(k)}\|}{\|\varepsilon^{(0)}\|}\right).
\]
Norm contraction implies nondecreasing $\mathcal P_k$. However, monotone decrease does not imply convergence to zero: $1+1/(n+1)$ is strictly decreasing and converges to $1$.

\section{A scale-invariant Navier--Stokes diagnostic}
Consider
\[
\varepsilon_{NS}(u)=\frac{\|(u\cdot\nabla)u\|_2}{\nu\|\Delta u\|_2},
\]
when the denominator is nonzero.
\begin{proposition}[Scaling invariance]
Under $u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u(\lambda x,\lambda^2t)$ on $\mathbb R^3$,
\[
\varepsilon_{NS}(u_\lambda)=\varepsilon_{NS}(u)
\]
whenever both sides are defined.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The nonlinear term scales pointwise as $\lambda^3[(u\cdot\nabla)u](\lambda x,\lambda^2t)$. Thus
\[
\|(u_\lambda\cdot\nabla)u_\lambda\|_2^2
=\lambda^6\lambda^{-3}\|(u\cdot\nabla)u\|_2^2,
\]
so its norm scales as $\lambda^{3/2}$. Likewise $\Delta u_\lambda=\lambda^3(\Delta u)(\lambda x,\lambda^2t)$ and $\|\Delta u_\lambda\|_2=\lambda^{3/2}\|\Delta u\|_2$. The factors cancel.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
Scaling invariance makes the diagnostic structurally interesting but does not imply a regularity criterion. A universal threshold requires an independent coercive estimate.
\end{remark}

\section{Phase decomposition and limitations}
For a pure mode $Ae^{i\omega t}$ with $\omega\ne0$, integration over an integer number of periods gives zero mean. This does not imply pointwise smallness; $\sin t$ has zero period mean but unit amplitude. Consequently phase cancellation cannot establish regularity without a theorem connecting the cancelled component to a recognized PDE norm.

\section{Counterexample library}
The audit records the following invalid inference patterns:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item monotone error reduction $\not\Rightarrow$ convergence to zero;
\item zero time average $\not\Rightarrow$ pointwise control;
\item scaling invariance $\not\Rightarrow$ regularity criterion;
\item finite computational verification $\not\Rightarrow$ universal quantification;
\item normalized percentages $\not\Rightarrow$ model-independent probabilities.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Critical-space bridge}
A decisive target is a theorem of the form
\[
\text{repository control condition}
\Longrightarrow
u\in L^\infty(0,T;L^3(\mathbb R^3)),
\]
where the right-hand velocity symbol is understood as $u$. Under appropriate classical hypotheses, endpoint $L^3$ control supplies a regularity mechanism. The first implication is currently an open bridge in this audit.

\section{Formal verification architecture}
The repository contains Lean and Coq material and audit-lemma counterparts in Coq, Isabelle/HOL and Agda. Formal status is theorem-specific. A proof is classified as kernel-checked only after exact compilation with transitive assumptions audited.

\section{Provenance methodology}
For significant historical objects the audit preserves or seeks full commit SHA, parent SHA, tree SHA, blob SHA, author and committer dates, immutable URLs and raw-byte SHA-256. Zenodo/DOI metadata is a separate archival layer. Git chronology establishes repository history but not worldwide mathematical priority.

\section{Research operating environment}
The audit is implemented as a typed research hub. Numerical outputs carry epistemic statuses such as `MODEL\_DERIVED`, `MATHEMATICAL\_DERIVATION`, `DIAGNOSTIC\_NOT\_REGULARITY\_THEOREM`, and `OPEN\_BRIDGE`. This prevents calculator results from being silently promoted to theorem status.

\section{Discussion}
The reconstructed corpus exhibits a coherent progression from residual obstruction to measurable gap, from gap to parameterized error, and from scalar error to vector/phase representations. The progression is historically meaningful and computationally useful. The main unresolved scientific question is whether one of these diagnostics controls a classical critical regularity quantity with sufficient uniformity.

\section{Conclusion}
The project is presently strongest as a provenance-aware mathematical audit and research operating environment. Several internal identities are proved and executable; the Navier--Stokes diagnostic is scale invariant; and outstanding theorem obligations are explicit. The classical Millennium conclusion remains open until the repository-to-critical-space bridge is proved.
