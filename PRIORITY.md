# Priority vs Mathematical Validity

Chronological evidence can establish that specific Fagliari framework components predate 8 September 2026.

It does **NOT** by itself establish:
- that OpenAI accessed the material,
- that OpenAI derived its construction from it,
- authorship of the OpenAI construction,
- plagiarism,
- prize entitlement,
- validity of Fagliari's unproved conjectures.

The mathematical audit is independent of the priority audit.
